Franchise Opportunity Profile

Tropical Smoothie Cafe

Tropical Smoothie Cafe is a better-for-you fast-casual franchise founded in 1997 in Destin, Florida. Every fact on this page is sourced from the TSC Franchisor, LLC Franchise Disclosure Document (issued April 17, 2026; amended June 10, 2026), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$35,000

First restaurant; $25,000 per additional restaurant; $17,500 for qualified veterans (VetFran); non-refundable. (FDD Item 5, p.9)

Estimated Initial Investment

$275,500 to $770,500

End-cap or in-line location; includes $45,000 to franchisor/affiliate (fee + $10,000 grand opening contribution). Drive-thru adds $40,000-$60,000. (FDD cover, p.i; Item 7, p.18)

Royalty

6% of Gross Sales

Payable weekly for the prior week's Gross Sales by electronic funds transfer. (FDD Item 6, p.10)

National Marketing Fee

5% of Gross Sales (up to 6%)

Current fee is 5%; franchisor may raise it to 6% in its sole discretion. (FDD Item 6, p.10)

Territory

Non-exclusive, 1/2-mile Protected Area

No exclusive territory; protected radius around your cafe; franchisor reserves Reserved-Facility and alternative-channel rights. (FDD Item 12, pp.42-43)

Term

15 years + one 10-year renewal

No renewal fees; must sign then-current agreement; franchisor may terminate only for cause (10-day cure on monetary defaults). (FDD Item 17, p.49)

Owner Model

Semi-absentee allowed

Not required to work in the business; entity owners must designate an Operating Principal (>=5% ownership); approved on-site manager required. (FDD Item 15, p.48)

HQ

Atlanta, Georgia

TSC Franchisor, LLC, 1117 Perimeter Center West, Suite W200, Atlanta, GA 30338. (770) 821-1900. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

1,651 outlets

1,650 franchised + 1 company-owned as of 12/28/2025; net +136 in 2025, +143 in 2024, +174 in 2023. (FDD Item 20, p.59)

Ownership

Blackstone-backed

Investment funds managed by affiliates of Blackstone, Inc. acquired TSC June 10, 2024. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

Litigation

2 disclosed matters, both settled

2016 franchisee arbitration (settled $62,500); 2025 franchisee bankruptcy adversary proceeding (settled 12/31/2025). No bankruptcy disclosed. (FDD Items 3-4, pp.8-9)

Founded

1997, Destin, Florida

Franchising began 1997 (TSFDC area rights); TSC began offering franchises in 2012; TSC Franchisor LLC took over Sept. 6, 2024. (FDD Item 1, pp.1-2)
